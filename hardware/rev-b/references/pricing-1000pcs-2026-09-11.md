# Battery PCB pricing worksheet — 1,000 boards — 2026-09-11

Scope: the revised schematic in `hardware/rev-b/cost-revision/design.json`, with the two SHOU HAN USB-C sourcing candidates already investigated in the design task, and the user's generic holder allowance. This is a pricing worksheet, not an assembly BOM or approved substitution list.

## Basis and result

- Quantity: 1,000 boards. Buy quantity for a shared MPN equals its per-board quantity × 1,000; the applicable published tier is used. No rounding up to full reels, attrition or excess inventory included.
- Currency: USD catalogue prices × INR96/USD, the existing project planning assumption, not a live exchange-rate claim.
- Source: PCBParts `jlc_get_part` responses retrieved this turn. These expose LCSC-linked catalogue tier prices and stock. JLC catalogue membership does not establish the same price or stock in JLC assembly inventory. The JLC public USB connector page confirms the catalogue entry, but did not expose a purchase price in the retrieved page.
- Priced subset: **USD2.4084 / INR231.21 per board**.
- Generic holder: **INR30 per board allowance**, using the user's confirmation that it can be sourced below INR30; no supplier quote was independently obtained.
- Priced subset plus holder: **INR261.21 per board / INR261206.40 for 1,000 boards**.
- Not included: all unresolved rows below, bare PCB, assembly/setup/attrition, programming, production test, shipping or taxes.
- This subset already exceeds the INR200 factory-cost ceiling. A complete production total cannot be established from the current unresolved schematic.
